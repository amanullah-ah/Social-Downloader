Questions, answered

Is it free?

Yes. Paste a link and download without paying or creating an account.

Why does my link fail?

Private videos, deleted posts and some age-restricted content can’t be fetched. Check that the video opens without logging in.

Where are my files saved?

In your device’s normal downloads folder, or where your browser asks you to save them.

Is downloading allowed?

Only save videos you own or have permission to use. Respect each platform’s terms and the creator’s copyright.
